# Rebuilding the figures

Model diagrams are generated by `scripts/generate-figures.mjs`. It uses Node.js built-ins;
no packages, browser, network access, or TikZ installation are required.

From the repository root:

```sh
node scripts/generate-figures.mjs
node scripts/generate-figures.mjs --check
```

- The first command rebuilds the SVGs in `diagrams/`: `system-overview.svg`,
  `dome-contact.svg`, `dome-dimensions.svg`, `foam-curve-anchors.svg`, and
  `foam-curve-two-anchors.svg`.
- `--check` leaves files unchanged and fails if any differs from its source.
  Both commands also check the curve's zero pressure, fitted anchor, prediction
  at the unused point, continuity, and increasing resistance.
- Output paths are relative to the script, so it also works from another directory.

## Where to edit

- **Dome diagram:** `domeContactSvg()` contains the editable SVG drawing code.
  This is a schematic, not a calculated contact shape or a TikZ figure.
- **Dome dimensions:** `domeDimensionsSvg()` draws the 100 mm curvature radius
  and 80 mm face width at a common scale. The face edge is calculated from the
  circle geometry; a check verifies that it lies on the circle.
- **Curve graph:** `reference`, `fitAnchor`, and the pressure functions define
  the estimated VA35 curve. `checkPoint` is kept out of the fit and used only
  for comparison. `foamCurveSvg()` supplies axes, markers, and labels. Curve
  paths are calculated from the equations on every run.
- **Two-anchor graph:** `twoAnchorPressure()` uses that same check point as a
  second input. `twoAnchorSvg()` compares this fit with the one-anchor estimate.
  The checks verify both anchors, continuity, and agreement below the first point.
- `tailPlot` sets both graphs' 0-100% compression axis and 1,500 kPa display
  ceiling. `cappedTailCurve()` stops each line where it crosses the ceiling;
  it never evaluates 100% strain or flattens the pressure curve. Beyond 80% is
  marked as extrapolation. Both plots continue the EVA equation itself, without
  a density-based extension.
- Source citations and pressure units are recorded at the top of the script.
  If changing the example, review its labels and the accompanying `MODEL.md` text.

Edit the generator, regenerate, and inspect the figures. Keep both the generator
and SVG outputs in version control; the Markdown document embeds the SVGs.

## Analysis figures and recipe selection

Analyses 1-5 use `data/simulations.csv` by default. They require
Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and the simulation's PyYAML runtime. Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m analysis.analysis1
python3 -m analysis.analysis2
python3 -m analysis.analysis4
python3 -m analysis.analysis3
python3 -m analysis.analysis5
python3 -m analysis.analysis6
python3 -m scripts.render_analysis
```

- `analysis/analysis1.py` writes four display-ready CSV tables used by the
  permanent table directives in Section 2. `scripts/render_analysis.py` expands
  those directives into `docs/ANALYSIS_rendered.md` without changing the authored file.
  `analysis/analysis2.py` writes the response bands and plots force at 5 ms by
  main-cushion family for Section 3.
- Analysis 4 selects both recipes and directly reruns the engine for their response
  plots. It writes the two performance tables into Section 6, along with
  composition tables, per-stack scores, `table4-scoring.json`, and
  `table4-{pillowy,poppy}-recipe.json` in `figures/`.
  The filenames retain their original style names; visible labels are Soft and Responsive.
- Analysis 3 reads those recipe files and validates their layers, impact conditions,
  and outcomes against the CSV before placing markers. Its 0.5 g quintile bands
  are descriptive; each frontier follows the lower edge, including early response.
  The recipe scores use continuous percentiles within 2.50 +/- 0.05 g.
- Analysis 5 plots peak force against force at 5 ms for Section 5, with weight
  encoded by color and the selected recipes marked for reference.
- Analysis 6 reads the Analysis 4 recipes and tests proportional glove thicknesses
  of 25-60 mm at 60-120 J, keeping striking mass and target fixed. It writes
  `figure6-*.png` in `figures/` and its results CSV, full compressed engine outputs,
  input snapshots, manifest and validation in `data/table6-sensitivity/`.
  Its authored conclusions and figures belong in Section 7 of `docs/ANALYSIS.md`.
  Rerunning reuses raw cases only when the request and simulation inputs match.
  Use `python3 -m analysis.analysis6 --plot-only` to redraw the saved results, or a new
  `--data-dir` when recipes or simulation inputs change.
- Analyses 3-5 accept `--results` and `--output-dir`. Use the same values throughout.
  Analysis 4 also accepts `--recipe-weight`, `--recipe-weight-tolerance`,
  `--peak-force-limit-kn`, and `--percentile`.
- The default protection ceiling is the original soft recipe's 3.026110963 kN.
  Percentile reference ranks are calculated before applying that ceiling.
  Before selection, require force at 5 ms below 0.50 kN for soft or at least
  0.70 kN for responsive; 0.50 to below 0.70 kN is neutral. The boundaries live in
  `analysis/analysis3.py` and are shared by the histogram and recipe selection.
  Scores use 60% protection, 20% compression reserve, and 20% early response;
  lower values score higher for all three metrics after applying the response
  class constraints.
  `table4-weight-sensitivity.csv` compares 50/20/30 and 70/20/10 with that default.
- Rebuild Analysis 4 before Analyses 3 and 5 whenever data or scoring changes.
  Its manifest records the input CSV hash, population, conditions, and selection rules;
  recipe files also record engine, catalog, assumptions, analysis-code, and shared-definition hashes.
- Analysis 6 checks exact baseline replays and doubled numerical resolution at
  eight grid corners. `validation.json` and `refinement.csv` retain the checks;
  `finite-changes.csv` retains the equal-step thickness and energy comparisons.
- Previous outputs and table scripts are preserved in
  `figures/archive/pre-5ms-2026-10-24/`.

Review `docs/ANALYSIS.md` after rebuilding; its conclusions are authored, not generated.
