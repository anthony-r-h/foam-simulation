Protocol fr50-eva50-v1: what gets tested

Exactly four different inventory materials, one per role, totaling 50 mm. Hand comfort and support backing each use 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 mm. Outer contact and main padding each use a positive multiple of 5 mm and fill the remainder. No material repeats across roles; no role contains a mixture.

All seven inventory materials are eligible for every role. The 39 thickness patterns and 840 material assignments give 32,760 role assignments. Equivalent material/thickness compositions are combined into 7,910 numbered tests because the model ignores roles and layer order. All equivalent role assignments are retained in the CSV.

Every test uses 50 mm EVA75 on a fixed plate, the adult impact (2.597 kg at 7.7 m/s), zero gravity and the fixed 1 kN/mm hand spring. Stress scaling 1, tail power 2, full thickness retention and firmness 1 stay fixed. Sample weight uses 30 × 30 mm.

F10/Fmax = F(min(10 ms, time to peak))/Fmax: an earlier peak gives 100%. Energy at exactly 10 ms is remaining striker kinetic energy, unavailable when the loading trace ends earlier. Conclusions apply only to this model and grid.

Run or update the study to save the CSV. Cancellation preserves recorded tests; unrun tests are not analyzed. The command-line runner also archives the full plan and model source with SHA-256 hashes for reproducibility.
